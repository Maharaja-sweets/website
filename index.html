Our Story

Since 1988

For over three decades, Maharaja Sweets has been crafting traditional Indian sweets and snacks the way they were always meant to be made — with pure ghee, time-honoured recipes, and an unwavering devotion to quality. What began as a small family kitchen has grown into a name trusted across Tamil Nadu.

Every Kaju Katli, every Mysorepak, every Laddu that leaves our kitchen carries the same care we put into the very first box we ever sold. We believe sweetness isn't just a flavour — it's a feeling, a memory, a tradition passed from one generation to the next.
